Jalen Hurts

Jalen Hurts is the star quarterback of the Philadelphia Eagles.
He is known for his leadership, athleticism, and ability to win under pressure.
From his college days at Alabama and Oklahoma to becoming a Super Bowl MVP, Hurts inspires fans with his determination and skill.

Career Highlights

  1. Super Bowl Champion & MVP (2025)
  2. 2× Pro Bowl Selection
  3. SEC Offensive Player of the Year (2016)
  4. CFP National Champion (2017)
  5. Most rushing TDs in a season by a QB (15)

Skill and impact

Hurts’ signature dual-threat ability makes him a formidable weapon. He can extend plays with his legs, escape pressure in the pocket, and keep defenses off-balance with his rushing talent. As he has progressed, his passing game has become more consistent, demonstrating a high football IQ and improved decision-making. His leadership qualities, combined with his dedication to personal improvement, have made him a respected leader in the locker room and a key driver of the Eagles' success.

Playoff Success and Super Bowl Glory

In the NFL, Jalen Hurts has consistently elevated his performance during the playoffs. He guided the Eagles to the Super Bowl in 2023 and again in 2025. During Super Bowl LIX in 2025, Hurts delivered a masterful performance that earned him Super Bowl MVP honors. He threw for two touchdowns and set a Super Bowl record for rushing yards by a quarterback, leading the Eagles to a 40-22 victory over the Kansas City Chiefs. His poise and playmaking on the biggest stage cemented his status as a franchise quarterback.
